# Plows

Pick your plow in the Garage. Every plow has the same base kit (slam, ram, dash strike, whirlwind) plus its own F
ability, a four-upgrade line in the Garage, and an **ultimate**. ARSENAL is the exception: it replaces the base kit
with guns.

**⚙ Settings → Main attacks:** each vehicle can be switched from SPECIAL (its own SPACE / E) to SNOWBALL: it gets a
blade, plows snow into a ball, slams with SPACE and whirls with E like the Classic, and keeps its own F and ultimate.
It takes effect on the next run; the Duel always uses SPECIAL.

## Classic Plow

+25% HP and full slam damage. **F: Inferno**, a free fire cone that burns bots and ends in a fireball.

Line: Inferno Exhaust (hotter, faster fire), Shatter Slam (slams throw ice shards at bots around the impact), Snow
Reserve (keep part of your ball after a slam), Slam Combo (back-to-back slams hit harder, stacking up to 3 times).

**Ultimate: FIRESTORM.** Fire damage fills the meter; when it's full, F fires a 360° fire nova that leaves burning
ground.

## VOLT-9

In the Duel its attacks hit 1.8x as hard (its kit is built for crowds).

No blade: a low, wide rover under a crackling coil tower. It doesn't plow snow or carry a snowball, though deep
snow still slows it down. +12% speed, +25% acceleration and +20% dash, +15% damage, but -15% HP.

- **SPACE (hold): Arc Lance.** Every 0.15 s an arc jumps from the tower to the nearest bot within 60° of the nose and
  9 m (Mega Hopper adds reach), then chains to 2 more bots nearby at half damage. It stays locked onto its target
  while that bot stays in reach. Overclock speeds the arc up.
- **E: Static Field.** An electrified field rides with the rover for 2.5 s (6.5 m, wider with Tesla Field). It pulses
  every 0.5 s, the first pulse a bigger burst: each pulse damages and stuns every bot inside for 0.7 s, so bots caught in
  it stay locked down while you drive through a pack. It recharges on the E timer (Whirlwind Turbine and Overclock
  shorten it).
- **F: Thunderclap.** A free lightning storm that costs a little HP. The self-shock escalates fast if you chain-spam
  it, and the meter over the F icon shows the next clap's cost.

Line: Chain Lightning (also adds Arc Lance hops: +1 per 2 arcs), Tesla Field (passive aura, and a wider Static Field),
Thunderclap (its damage also overcharges Arc Lance and Static Field), Battery Shield, Arc Lance (the primary's own
line: every level +10% arc damage, chain hops included, and +5% arcs per second, the rate tapering past level 5).
Packed Ice raises all of it.

**Ultimate: SUPERCELL.** Any damage fills the meter. Three waves of sky lightning hit everything within 18 m, with
no Overload backlash.

## BRUTUS

A wrecking mech: tracks, a hunched armoured torso, two piston fists and a flat buzzsaw on each front corner.
No blade, no snowball (snow still drags on the tracks). +35% HP and +20% damage, but -8% acceleration and -10% dash.

- **SPACE (hold): Wrecking Fist.** Punch, punch, finisher, one every 0.26 s with **no cooldown**: the combo loops for
  as long as you hold SPACE. The bot nearest the fist line takes the full hit, the rest of the fist's arc 35%. The
  finisher (both fists) hits 2.2x, stuns 0.5 s, hurls the bot away and sends a **shockwave** rolling 8 m on down a
  lane ahead, hitting every bot in it for a jab's damage and shoving it back. Fists reach 4 m past the hull. In the Duel BRUTUS hits at 0.7x so it stays fair.
- **E: Saw Charge.** A 0.6 s forward charge (17 m/s) with both saws screaming: every bot around the nose is shredded
  every 0.1 s and flung aside. Recharge = the E (Whirlwind Turbine) cooldown.
- **F: Ground Pound**, a free leap you can't be hit during, then an AoE damage ring with knockback and stun.
- DASH (L-SHIFT) as everyone.

Upgrade mapping: Mega Hopper = bigger fists (reach and damage); Momentum = harder fists (grows with the square of
the level) plus rams / dash strikes scaled by speed; Buzzsaws = Saw Charge damage (+half its dps) and reach;
Whirlwind Turbine = Saw Charge damage and recharge; Iron Hide and Ground Pound unchanged.

Line: Ground Pound, Buzzsaws, Iron Hide, Momentum, Piston Fists (the primary's own line: every level +10% punch damage,
finisher and splash included, and +0.3 m fist reach, tapering past level 5), Spiked Hull (passive: bots within about
3.4 m of the mech take 7+ dps, x your damage mult, ticking every 0.3 s, and get shoved away; sparks on the hull show
it).

**Ultimate: EARTHQUAKE.** Any damage fills the meter. Three shockwaves roll out to 19 m (1.07x pound damage each, a little more as Ground Pound is upgraded).

## ISOTOPE ☢️

A long, low nuclear flatbed: a stubby armoured cab, a big glowing reactor capsule lying on the bed with control rods
bobbing out of it, twin cooling stacks, a spinning vent fan and a sprayer nozzle over the nose. No blade, no snowball
(deep snow still slows it). +5% speed and +10% HP; bots it irradiates glow green, take +30% damage from you
and burn (radiation damage every 0.5 s while it lasts, 3 s after the last dose).

- **SPACE (hold): Rad Sprayer.** Every 0.12 s (faster with Overclock) the nozzle hoses a glowing cone (about 70°
  wide, 8.5 m + Mega Hopper reach). The nearest bot in it takes the full dose, every other bot in the cone 85%; all
  of them get irradiated. Half of the spray's damage counts toward Critical Mass.
- **E: Fallout Trail.** For 4 s the reactor dumps coolant: a toxic puddle every 0.3 s behind you (about 2 m wide,
  3.5 s each) that doses and irradiates every bot driving through it and grips it for a moment (once per 0.3 s,
  overlaps don't stack). Recharge = the E
  (Whirlwind Turbine) cooldown.
- **F: Meltdown**, a free toxic blast that melts the snow and leaves a glowing fallout pool.
- DASH (L-SHIFT) as everyone.

Line: Meltdown (also overheats the reactor: Rad Sprayer and Fallout Trail hit harder, steeply at high levels),
Half-Life (bigger, hotter, longer pools and wider, hotter trail puddles), Rad Aura (passive radiation around you),
Chain Reaction (irradiated bots explode when wrecked, setting off their neighbours), Rad Sprayer (the primary's own
line: every level +10% spray dose, splash included, and +0.5 m reach, tapering past level 5).

Upgrade mapping: Packed Ice = all damage; Mega Hopper = Rad Sprayer reach; Whirlwind Turbine = Fallout Trail recharge
and heat; Snow Compactor = puddles linger longer (up to 2x); Avalanche Core = 50% wider spray cone; Frost Core = the
puddle sludge holds bots (0.6 s per tick).

**Ultimate: NUKE.** Radiation damage fills the ☢ Critical Mass bar; when it's full, F fires an instant huge blast
(2.6x Meltdown damage) and a giant fallout pool.

## ARSENAL 🎯

Not a plow at all: a battle tank with no blade and no snowball (+10% speed, -10% HP). **Every weapon hits bots 3x as
hard** (Campaign, Survival, Brawl); in the Duel it fights at normal strength. Crawler tracks under armoured
skirts (the treads scroll and the sprockets turn as it drives), a sloped glacis with the laser projector set into it,
a six-sided turret carrying the long pulse-laser cannon, a commander's cupola with a whip antenna that rocks back when
it surges, and box missile launchers on the rear fenders. Snow slows it like any other vehicle; DASH is shared.

- **Space (hold): pulse laser.** A turret laser cannon firing 12+ red laser bolts a second that auto-aims at the best
  target inside a **240° arc centred on the hull's nose** (bots squarely behind you are out of reach) and tilts from
  -35° to +45°, preferring bots near the nose. The turret swings at a steady 3.2 rad/s and only fires once it is on
  target, so switching between far-apart bots costs a moment.
- **E: laser beam.** A 1.5 s beam that burns through everything in a line and tracks its target a little.
- **F: swarm missiles.** A salvo of homing missiles that each hunt a different bot, ranking targets by their HP and
  the damage they've dealt you.

Line: Pulse Laser, Laser Lance, Swarm Missiles, Energy Barrier (a recharging shield, since there's no ball to hide
behind), Scrap Magnet (passive: a magnetic field, 7 m and wider with levels, does a
trickle of damage to bots inside, about a quarter of Rad Aura before the PvE bonus; every bot you wreck within 12 m sheds
a scrap shard that flies in and orbits the hull 3.5 m out for 15 s, up to 4 + level shards (12 at most); each shard
that passes through a bot hits it, each bot at most every 0.4 s per shard; all of it gets the 3x PvE bonus against
bots, and in the Duel only the field and shards from wrecks count).

The snowball lines still pay off on the tank: Packed Ice is the damage multiplier for every weapon, Mega Hopper adds
pulse-laser reach (+5 m per extra metre of max ball radius), Whirlwind Turbine speeds the turret traverse and the laser
beam's bend (+35% per extra metre of spin radius) and Snow Compactor widens the laser beam.

**Ultimate: MISSILE STORM.** Any damage fills the meter. Every pod empties: 21 heavy missiles ripple out over about a
second and spread over the biggest threats up to 75 m away (a normal salvo reaches 45 m). Each hits for 4× missile
damage in a bigger blast.

## Ultimates and the kill-cam

The ultimate meter sits under your HP bar, and the F button changes when it's full. An ultimate's own damage never
refills its meter. When one ultimate scores its 3rd kill within 4.5 s, the game drops into slow motion for about a
second, the camera swings out to frame the wreckage, and a banner shows the tally ("💥 x5 ULTIMATE WIPE"). This plays
in the Campaign, Survival and Brawl, never in the Tutorial or the Duel, and can be turned off in ⚙ Settings
(**Ultimate kill-cam**).
