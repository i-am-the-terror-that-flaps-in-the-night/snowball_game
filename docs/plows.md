# Plows

Pick your plow in the Garage. Every plow has the same base kit (slam, ram, dash strike, whirlwind) plus its own F
ability, a four-upgrade line in the Garage, and an **ultimate**. ARSENAL is the exception: it replaces the base kit
with magnets and lasers.

**⚙ Settings → Main attacks:** each vehicle can be switched from SPECIAL (its own SPACE / E) to SNOWBALL: it gets a
blade, plows snow into a ball, slams with SPACE and whirls with E like the Classic, and keeps its own F and ultimate.
It takes effect on the next run; the Duel always uses SPECIAL.

## Classic Plow

+25% HP and full slam damage. Past Packed Ice level 4, each further level adds another +5.5% damage on the Classic
(about +33% at Packed Ice 10, easing toward +66% at very high levels), so its slams and fire keep up late game. **F: Inferno**, a free fire cone that
burns bots and ends in a fireball.

Line: Inferno Exhaust (hotter, faster fire), Shatter Slam (slams throw ice shards at bots around the impact), Snow
Reserve (keep part of your ball after a slam), Slam Combo (back-to-back slams hit harder, stacking up to 3 times; +24% per stack at level 3, +39% at level 8).

**Ultimate: FIRESTORM.** Fire damage fills the meter; when it's full, F fires a 360° fire nova that leaves burning
ground.

## VOLT-9

In the Duel its attacks hit 1.8x as hard (its kit is built for crowds).

No blade: a low, wide rover under a crackling coil tower. It doesn't plow snow or carry a snowball, though deep
snow still slows it down. +12% speed, +25% acceleration and +20% dash, +15% damage, but -5% HP.

- **SPACE (hold): Arc Lance.** Every 0.15 s an arc jumps from the tower to the nearest bot within 60° of the nose and
  9 m (Mega Hopper adds reach), then **chains a long way**: 6 more hops (plus one per Chain Lightning arc), each to the
  nearest unhit bot within 8 m, the first hop at 60% damage and each one after keeping 90% of the one before. Every
  arc hit (any hop) has a 7% chance to **PARALYSE** the bot for 1.2 s (a stun; bosses half); a paralysed bot can't be
  paralysed again for 2.5 s after. **GROUNDED ARC:** when the locked bot is a boss, or has nothing within 8 m to chain to
  (a straggler), the arc grounds into it for 1.75x damage (2.6x on a boss) and faces only half its armour; on a boss the
  chain still runs on to its adds. Bots only (the Duel is unchanged). It stays locked onto its target
  while that bot stays in reach. Overclock speeds the arc up.
- **E: Static Field.** An electrified field rides with the rover for 2.5 s (6.5 m, wider with Tesla Field). It pulses
  every 0.5 s, the first pulse a bigger burst: each pulse damages and stuns every bot inside for 0.7 s, so bots caught in
  it stay locked down while you drive through a pack. It recharges on the E timer (Whirlwind Turbine and Overclock
  shorten it).
- **F: Thunderclap.** A free lightning storm that costs a little HP. The self-shock escalates fast if you chain-spam
  it, and the meter over the F icon shows the next clap's cost.

Line: Chain Lightning (also adds Arc Lance hops: +1 per 2 arcs), Tesla Field (passive aura, and a wider Static Field),
Thunderclap (its damage also overcharges Arc Lance and Static Field), Battery Shield, Arc Lance (the primary's own
line: every level +10% arc damage, chain hops included, and +5% arcs per second, the rate tapering past level 5).
Packed Ice raises all of it.

**Ultimate: SUPERCELL.** Any damage fills the meter. Three waves of sky lightning hit everything within 18 m, with
no Overload backlash.

## BRUTUS

A wrecking mech: tracks, a hunched armoured torso, two piston fists and a flat buzzsaw on each front corner.
No blade, no snowball (snow still drags on the tracks). +35% HP and +20% damage, but -8% acceleration and -10% dash.

- **SPACE (hold): Wrecking Fist.** Punch, punch, finisher, one every 0.26 s with **no cooldown**: the combo loops for
  as long as you hold SPACE. The bot nearest the fist line takes the full hit, the rest of the fist's arc 35%. The
  finisher (both fists) hits 2.2x, stuns 0.5 s, hurls the bot away and sends a **shockwave** rolling 8 m on down a
  lane ahead, hitting every bot in it for a jab's damage and shoving it back. Fists reach 4 m past the hull.
  **Solid hits, not snowballs:** every jab that lands square **staggers** the bot (0.18 s stun, it reels back), punches
  crunch through frontal plates (Brute Dozer / Frostbyte front take 80% instead of 30% / 50%), and each landed jab gives
  a short meaty hit-stop with hot metal sparks, grit and dust at the contact point (no white puffs). In the Duel BRUTUS hits at 0.7x so it stays fair.
- **E: Saw Charge.** A 0.6 s forward charge (17 m/s) with both saws screaming: every bot around the nose is shredded
  every 0.1 s and flung aside. Recharge = the E (Whirlwind Turbine) cooldown.
- **F: Ground Pound**, a free leap you can't be hit during, then an AoE damage ring with knockback and stun.
- DASH (L-SHIFT) as everyone.

Upgrade mapping: Mega Hopper = bigger fists (reach and damage); Momentum = harder fists (grows with the square of
the level) plus rams / dash strikes scaled by speed; Buzzsaws = Saw Charge damage (+half its dps) and reach;
Whirlwind Turbine = Saw Charge damage and recharge; Iron Hide and Ground Pound unchanged.

Line: Ground Pound, Buzzsaws, Iron Hide, Momentum, Piston Fists (the primary's own line: every level +10% punch damage,
finisher and splash included, and +0.3 m fist reach, tapering past level 5), Spiked Hull (passive: bots within about
3.4 m of the mech take 7+ dps, x your damage mult, ticking every 0.3 s, and get shoved away; sparks on the hull show
it).

**Ultimate: EARTHQUAKE.** Any damage fills the meter. Three shockwaves roll out to 19 m (1.07x pound damage each, a little more as Ground Pound is upgraded).

## ISOTOPE ☢️

A long, low nuclear flatbed: a stubby armoured cab, a big glowing reactor capsule lying on the bed with control rods
bobbing out of it, twin cooling stacks, a spinning vent fan and a sprayer nozzle over the nose. No blade, no snowball
(deep snow still slows it). +5% speed and +20% HP; bots it irradiates glow green, take +30% damage from you
and burn (radiation damage every 0.5 s while it lasts, 3 s after the last dose, or for as long as a RAD DOSE is
still soaking in). **Lead lining:** a bot it has irradiated hits ISOTOPE 20% softer (bots only; Duel and Brawl
unchanged), so dosing a pack before wading into it pays off.

- **SPACE (hold): Rad Sprayer.** Every 0.12 s (faster with Overclock) the nozzle hoses a glowing cone (about 100°
  wide, 8.5 m + Mega Hopper reach). The nearest bot in it takes the full dose, every other bot in the cone 65%; all
  of them get irradiated. **RAD DOSE (extended radiation):** half of a spray hit lands on contact; the rest soaks
  in as a dose (x1.4 for the wait) that keeps burning long after you stop spraying or move on — a slice every 0.5 s
  (a fifth of what's left, plus a small floor), roughly 8-15 s of burn after a good soak — and the bot stays
  irradiated (+30% damage taken, base burn) while it carries a dose. Contagion: every burn leaks 20% into the nearest
  undosed bot within 3.5 m, so a dense pack keeps cooking. Half of the spray's damage counts toward Critical Mass.
  With lifesteal (Salvage, from Scrap Recycler) the dose's slice of each burn repairs the plow like a normal hit;
  the base radiation burn, puddles, pools and the aura still don't heal.
- **E: Fallout Trail.** For 4 s the reactor dumps coolant: a toxic puddle every 0.3 s behind you (about 2 m wide,
  3.5 s each) that doses and irradiates every bot driving through it and grips it for a moment (once per 0.3 s,
  overlaps don't stack). Recharge = the E
  (Whirlwind Turbine) cooldown.
- **F: Meltdown**, a free toxic blast that melts the snow and leaves a glowing fallout pool.
- DASH (L-SHIFT) as everyone.

Line: Meltdown (also overheats the reactor: Rad Sprayer and Fallout Trail hit harder, steeply at high levels),
Half-Life (bigger, hotter, longer pools and wider, hotter trail puddles), Rad Aura (passive radiation around you),
Chain Reaction (irradiated bots explode when wrecked, setting off their neighbours), Rad Sprayer (the primary's own
line: +25% spray dose per level for 3 levels, then +6% per level, splash included, and +0.5 m reach, tapering past
level 5).

Upgrade mapping: Packed Ice = all damage; Mega Hopper = Rad Sprayer reach; Whirlwind Turbine = Fallout Trail recharge
and heat; Snow Compactor = puddles linger longer (up to 2x); Avalanche Core = wider spray cone (about 120°); Frost Core = the
puddle sludge holds bots (0.6 s per tick).

**Ultimate: NUKE.** Radiation damage fills the ☢ Critical Mass bar; when it's full, F fires an instant huge blast
(2.6x Meltdown damage) and a giant fallout pool.

## ARSENAL 🎯

Not a plow at all: a futuristic magnetic / laser vehicle with no blade and no snowball (+10% speed, -10% HP). **Every
weapon hits bots 1.5x as hard** (Campaign, Survival, Brawl); in the Duel it fights at normal strength. Two huge rugged
off-road wheels carry the nose (armoured hubs, glowing cyan rims, they spin with the ground speed); there are no
wheels at the back: the rear hovers on a **gyro core**, three thin armoured rings nested like a gyroscope, each
spinning on its own axis round a small white-hot crystal, held in an armoured cradle between two short struts and
throwing faint light rings onto the snow. The rings spin up with speed and while the shotgun fires, and the rear
bobs gently. A sloped dark gunmetal hull sits between, with a low turret carrying the emitter (locked facing forward)
and the repulsor lens in the nose. Snow slows it like any other vehicle; DASH is shared. Like every plow it fights up
close and aims by steering: neither main weapon auto-aims.

- **Space (hold): Mag Shotgun.** The emitter is fixed straight ahead along the hull (it only tilts up to 20° to
  follow a bot on a slope). While held it fires a blast about every 0.55 s (faster with levels): 8 cyan pellets fanned
  across a 36° cone (Whirlwind Turbine adds more), and every bot inside the cone takes the WHOLE blast (no pellet luck). Short reach (~8 m, a
  little more with levels and Mega Hopper); pellets hit full out to 6 m and fall to 70% at full reach, so it is
  deadliest point-blank. A point-blank blast shoves the bot back; further out it only nudges. (The Magnetic Reel that
  dragged survivors into point-blank was removed: it kept ARSENAL out of reach of everything.) All of one blast's pellets on a bot land as one hit
  (one armour check; in the Duel it is soft-capped as one hit, so even a point-blank blast can't one-shot).
- **E: Mag Repulsor.** A magnetic wave bursts out of the nose across a 60° wedge (Snow Compactor widens it, 90° at
  most) and sweeps out to ~11 m in 0.3 s: a cyan arc wall racing over the snow, field-line streaks and a gyro flare.
  Every bot in the wedge takes one big hit as the front passes (full damage out to half its reach, 70% at the edge;
  the old Grav Lance's lone-bot damage) and is flung away from you (bosses barely move). No aiming help, no lock: point
  the nose at the pack. In the Duel it shoves the other pilot a moderate amount and hits for 60% (one big hit gets no
  tick-by-tick diminishing returns).
- **F: Polarity Burst.** The gyro rings snap flat into one spinning disc and a cyan event horizon opens 16 m round a
  singularity point 4 m ahead. For about a second every bot inside is dragged toward it (bosses less), streaking in;
  then it collapses and detonates: a cyan shockwave hits everything in the clump and flings it outward. A lone bot
  takes missile count x missile damage (the old salvo's worth); with more caught each share is thinner but the total
  bigger.

Line: Mag Shotgun, Mag Repulsor, Polarity Burst, Energy Barrier (a recharging shield, since there's no ball to hide
behind), Scrap Magnet (passive: a magnetic field, 7 m and wider with levels, does a
trickle of damage to bots inside, about a quarter of Rad Aura before the PvE bonus. **Strip scrap:** the ring doesn't
depend on wrecking cannon fodder: every 20% of a target's max HP your weapons tear off (shotgun, repulsor, burst,
singularity) rips a shard off it, bosses every 6% (at most 2 per hit), and every bot you wreck sheds one too; the ring's
own shard hits and the field count at a quarter rate (a ring grinding a boss slowly tops itself up, but stays full only
while you fight). A shard torn off within 12 m flies in and orbits the vehicle 3.5 m out for 15 s, up to 4 + level
shards (12 at most); each shard that passes through a bot hits it, each bot at most every 0.4 s per shard; all of it
gets the 1.5x PvE bonus against bots. In the Duel the opponent sheds shards the same way.) The line ids are the old ones (armCannon,
armLaser, armMissiles, ...), so saved levels carry over.

The snowball lines still pay off: Packed Ice is the damage multiplier for every weapon, Mega Hopper adds shotgun
reach (+1.5 m per extra metre of max ball radius), Whirlwind Turbine adds shotgun pellets (+1 per 2 m of extra spin
radius, 12 at most) and Snow Compactor widens the Mag Repulsor's wedge.

**Ultimate: SINGULARITY.** Any damage fills the meter. A black hole opens over the densest crowd within 40 m (or
12 m ahead when nothing is near): a true black core with a bright photon ring, gravitational lensing that bends the
arena behind it, and a tilted, swirling accretion disc. For 3 s it drags every bot within 40 m of it toward the core,
ever faster (bosses less), with small damage ticks and a building rumble; then it shrinks to a point and explodes:
a blinding flash (toned down with Reduce flashing), a huge shockwave, big damage to everything within 40 m (full
within 12 m, half at the rim, thinner shares the more it catches) and everything flung outward.

## Ultimates and the kill-cam

The ultimate meter sits under your HP bar, and the F button changes when it's full. An ultimate's own damage never
refills its meter. When one ultimate scores its 3rd kill within 4.5 s, the game drops into slow motion for about a
second, the camera swings out to frame the wreckage, and a banner shows the tally ("💥 x5 ULTIMATE WIPE"). This plays
in the Campaign, Survival and Brawl, never in the Tutorial or the Duel, and can be turned off in ⚙ Settings
(**Ultimate kill-cam**).
